KaosKaki turns your Android phone into a SOCKS5 proxy server. Laptops, tablets and other devices on the same Wi-Fi network, hotspot or USB connection can browse and work through your phone's internet connection.

SIMPLE
• One tap to start or stop the proxy
• Every address the proxy can be reached on (Wi-Fi, hotspot, USB, Bluetooth) is listed with one-tap copy
• Step-by-step connection guide inside the app

RELIABLE
• Runs in the background with a status notification and a Stop button
• Optional CPU and Wi-Fi wake locks keep the proxy responsive with the screen off
• Supports IPv4, IPv6 and domain-name destinations

SECURE
• Optional username and password authentication
• No accounts, no ads, no analytics: nothing is collected or sent to us

INSIGHTFUL
• Live statistics: active connections, data uploaded and downloaded, speed and uptime
• Activity log of every connection with destination, traffic and duration

HOW IT WORKS
1. Tap the power button in KaosKaki.
2. Connect the other device to the same network or to your phone's hotspot.
3. Set its proxy to SOCKS5 using the address and port shown in the app (default 1080).

Works with browsers such as Firefox, with Telegram, curl, git and other apps that support SOCKS5 proxies.

NOTES
• KaosKaki proxies TCP connections (SOCKS5 CONNECT). UDP-based apps such as voice calls and many games are not supported.
• Traffic through the proxy uses your phone's data plan. Check your carrier's terms for sharing your connection.
• Turn on authentication when using shared networks so others cannot use your connection.
